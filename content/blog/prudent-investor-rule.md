date: "2024-02-15"
---

The Prudent Investor Rule is a fundamental legal principle that guides fiduciary investment decisions. This rule, codified in many jurisdictions, sets the standard for responsible investment management.

## Core Components

The rule requires fiduciaries to:
- Act in the best interests of beneficiaries
- Exercise reasonable care, skill, and caution
- Design an investment portfolio that optimizes returns while managing acceptable risks

## Modern Portfolio Theory Integration

The Prudent Investor Rule aligns with Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT), emphasizing:
- Portfolio diversification
- Risk management
- Long-term investment perspective

Understanding and following these principles is crucial for anyone managing investments on behalf of others.
