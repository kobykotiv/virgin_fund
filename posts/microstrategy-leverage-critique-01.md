# MicroStrategy's Leverage: A Debt-Fueled Bet on Bitcoin?

MicroStrategy’s aggressive strategy of using leverage to buy Bitcoin has drawn criticism across the industry. Critics argue that their high debt levels not only increase risk but also resemble a Ponzi-like structure. While the company boasts impressive correlations between its stock and Bitcoin prices, this heavy reliance on debt for leveraging positions could backfire during market downturns.
