## Revenue Model
1. Platform Fees
   - 5% commission on bot rentals
   - 2% commission on copy trading
   - Premium features subscription

2. Provider Earnings
   - 95% of rental fees
   - 98% of copy trading fees
   - Analytics dashboard access
