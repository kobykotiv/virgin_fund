# Stock Screening for Value Investors: Finding Hidden Gems

Effective stock screening is crucial for value investors to identify potential investment opportunities. Here's how to build and use screening tools effectively.

## Fundamental Screening Criteria

### 1. Valuation Metrics
- P/E ratio range
- Price-to-book value
- EV/EBITDA multiple
- Free cash flow yield
- Dividend yield

### 2. Financial Health
- Debt-to-equity ratio
- Current ratio
- Interest coverage
- Return on equity
- Operating margins

### 3. Quality Indicators
- Operating history
- Earnings stability
- Revenue growth
- Cash flow consistency
- Market position
